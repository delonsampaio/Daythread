Trip & Itinerary Builder — Support & Help

Getting Started

How do I create my first trip?

Tap the Trips tab, then tap the + button. Fill in the trip name, destination, start and end dates, and an optional cover photo — all on one screen. Tap Save and the app switches to your new trip in the Timeline.

How do I switch between trips?

Tap the trip name in the centre of the Timeline navigation bar — it shows a dropdown of all your active trips. The current trip's date range appears just below the name so you always know which trip you're viewing.

How do I add an event to my itinerary?

In the Timeline, tap the blue + button in the bottom-right corner to open the Add Event sheet. Choose a category (Flight, Hotel, Restaurant, Museum, Activity, etc.), fill in the details, and save.

What categories of events can I add?

Daythread supports: Flight, Train, Bus, Ferry (transit), Hotel, Rental Property, Restaurant, Café, Bar, Museum, Attraction, Tour, Show, Activity, Sport, Hike, Shopping, and Other. Transit categories open an extended form for carrier, flight/train number, booking code, and seat.

How do I reorder events in my day?

Long-press and drag any event card to reorder it. You can also drag events between days — drop onto a different day's section and it moves there automatically. Two things can block a drag: (1) Time-Locked events cannot be dragged and block drops that would violate their time slot; (2) if the dragged event's time overlaps an existing timed event on the target day, the drop is blocked with a warning vibration — edit the time first or use the edit sheet. If timed events end up out of order, their time label turns amber and a "Sort by Time" button appears in the day header.

What happens if I add an event that overlaps another?

When you save a timed event whose start–end window overlaps another timed event on the same day, Daythread shows an alert naming the conflicting event(s). You can tap "Adjust Time" to return to the form and fix the times, or "Save Anyway" to keep the overlap — for example, when two people are splitting up for part of the day.

How do I edit or delete an event?

Swipe left on any event card to reveal three actions: Edit, Lock/Unlock, and Delete. Tap the action you want, or swipe right to dismiss. Time-Locked events show Unlock instead of Lock.

Timeline

What is a Time-Locked event?

A Time-Locked event is anchored to its time slot. It cannot be dragged, and other events cannot be dragged past it — if you try, the card snaps back with a warning vibration. When you set a start time on a new event the lock is suggested automatically. An amber padlock icon appears on the card, and an amber warning badge appears if the event is currently out of order.

What does the lodging banner at the top show?

If you've added lodging that covers today's date, a banner appears at the top of the Timeline showing your current accommodation. It updates automatically as your check-in and check-out dates change.

How are transit events different from regular events?

Flight, Train, Bus, and Ferry events show a detailed card with carrier name, flight or train number, booking code, terminal and gate, seat number, baggage claim, and timezone-corrected times. Fill in the transit details form after choosing one of these categories.

Can I add notes to an event?

Yes. The Add/Edit Event sheet has a notes field where you can save anything relevant — confirmation numbers, meeting points, dress codes, etc.

How do I add lodging to a trip?

From the Timeline, tap the + button and choose Hotel or Rental Property. Enter check-in and check-out dates, the property name, address, and confirmation number. Lodging covers the whole trip, not a single day.

Calendar & Notifications

Does Daythread sync my events to Apple Calendar?

Yes. When you save an event Daythread adds it to a dedicated "Daythread: [trip name]" calendar in your Apple Calendar app. The first sync asks for calendar permission. Turn this off globally in Profile → Settings → Calendar, or exclude a single event via the "Add to Apple Calendar" toggle in the Add/Edit Event sheet.

Why is there a "Daythread: [trip name]" calendar in Apple Calendar?

Daythread creates one calendar per trip so your itinerary stays separate from personal events. If you stop using the app you can delete those calendars from the iOS Calendar app at any time.

Can I exclude a specific event from Apple Calendar?

Yes. Open the Add/Edit Event sheet for the event and turn off the "Add to Apple Calendar" toggle. The event stays in Daythread but is removed from (or never added to) Apple Calendar. This is device-local — co-editors on the same trip control their own calendars independently.

Can I get reminders before my events?

Yes. Enable event reminders in Profile → Settings → Notifications and choose your lead time: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day before. Two reminders fire at the chosen time: a Daythread app notification and an Apple Calendar alarm. The lead time setting applies to both.

What can I do from a reminder notification?

Each reminder has two quick actions: Snooze 15 min (reschedules 15 minutes later without opening the app) and View Itinerary (opens Daythread to the trip timeline). Reminders group by trip on the lock screen so they stack neatly instead of flooding your notifications.

Daythread notifications aren't coming through during Focus mode. Why?

Daythread sends time-sensitive notifications designed to break through Focus modes. Go to iOS Settings → Focus → [your Focus] → Apps and add Daythread to the allowed list. If you still don't see them, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Daythread and ensure alerts and sounds are enabled.

Does Daythread look different on iPad?

Yes. On iPad in full-screen or wide Split View the four sections (Timeline, Trips, Vault, Profile) appear in a collapsible sidebar on the left with icons and labels. On iPhone and in narrow Stage Manager windows the standard bottom tab bar is used. Both layouts show exactly the same content.

Trips

How are trips organised?

The Trips tab groups your trips into Current, Upcoming, Past, and Archived. A trip is "Current" if today falls within its start and end dates, "Upcoming" if it's in the future, and "Past" after it ends.

How do I edit a trip?

Long-press any trip card in the Trips list and tap Edit. You can change the name, destination, cover photo, and dates. If you shorten the date range and any events fall outside the new dates, the app warns you with an exact count before deleting them.

How do I archive a trip?

Long-press any trip card in the Trips list and tap Archive. Archived trips are hidden from the Timeline trip picker and the Vault, but remain accessible in the Archived section of the Trips tab so you can refer back to them.

How do I delete a trip?

Long-press any trip card in the Trips list and tap Delete. This permanently removes the trip and all its days, events, documents, and expenses.

What are Pre-Trip Tasks?

Pre-Trip Tasks are a checklist you can attach to any trip for things to do before you leave — booking insurance, printing documents, packing specific items, etc. Tap a trip in the Trips tab and scroll to the Tasks section.

Can I add a cover photo to a trip?

Yes. The trip creation form has a Cover Photo field where you can pick one from your photo library. You can also change or remove it later — long-press the trip card and tap Edit, then use the Cover Photo section.

Vault (Documents & Expenses)

What can I store in the Vault?

The Vault holds two things: Documents (passports, visas, insurance cards, boarding passes, hotel confirmations — as PDFs or images) and Expenses (a running log of what you've spent per trip).

How do I add a document?

Go to Vault → Documents tab and tap +. Import from your Files app (PDF or image), take a photo with your camera, or pick from your photo library. Give it a title, an optional note (e.g. visa requirements, booking reference), and an optional expiry date.

Can I edit a document after adding it?

Yes — if you are the originator or the document is unlocked. Long-press the tile and tap Edit Details to change the title, notes, or expiry date. Documents are locked by default so only you can edit them; unlock from the same sheet to let others edit. The file itself cannot be replaced — delete and re-add it to swap the file.

Can I switch between a grid and a list view for documents?

Yes. Tap the grid/list toggle icon in the top-right toolbar of the Documents tab. The list view shows each document's thumbnail, title, notes preview, and expiry date on a single row — useful for quickly scanning details without opening each document. Your choice is remembered across sessions.

How do I sort documents?

Tap the ↑↓ sort icon in the Documents toolbar and choose one of four orders: Expiry (soonest first) — the default, floats expiring documents to the top; Name (A–Z); Date added (newest); or Date added (oldest).

Can I prevent others from editing my documents?

Yes. Every document has a Lock document toggle, which is on by default. When locked, only the person who added the document can change its title or expiry date — the Edit Details option is hidden for everyone else on the trip. As the originator you can unlock it at any time from Edit Details.

Can I share specific documents with trip co-editors? PRO

Yes. When adding or editing a document, use the Share with trip members toggle. When off (the default), only you can see the document. When on, all co-editors with access to the trip can view it. Only the originator of the document can change this setting.

How do I share a document?

Tap a document tile to open the full-screen viewer, then tap the share icon in the top-left corner to forward it via Messages, email, AirDrop, or any other app.

What does the expiry badge on a document tile mean?

A red badge means the document is expired or expires within 30 days — renew it immediately. An amber badge means it expires within 90 days, giving you time to act before most international entry requirements (many countries require a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your entry date).

How many documents can I store for free?

Free users can store up to 5 documents per trip. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited document storage.

How do I log an expense?

Go to Vault → Expenses tab and tap +. Enter the amount, currency, category (Food, Transport, Lodging, Activity, Shopping, or Other), date, and who paid. If participants are set up for the trip, selecting a payer is required. The Expenses tab shows a running total per currency.

Can I attach a receipt to an expense? PRO

Yes. Pro users can attach a receipt photo to any expense. When adding an expense, tap "Attach Receipt Photo" to take a photo with your camera or choose one from your photo library. A thumbnail appears on the expense row in the list — tap it to view the full receipt.

How do I split expenses with my travel group? PRO

In the Vault → Expenses tab, tap the ↻ icon in the top-right toolbar (Pro). Add participant names (e.g. "Me", "Alice", "Bob") — no app account required. When logging expenses, choose who paid and who to split it among. The app calculates the easiest way to settle debts with the fewest transactions.

If any expenses have no payer assigned, an orange warning appears at the top of the Settlements section — tap it to open Assign Payers and fix them in one place. Tap Settle on any debt row to record a payment.

Settlement payments appear in the expense list with a Settlement badge so you can see the full ledger history in one place. Swipe to delete a settlement row if it becomes outdated.

Can I settle a partial amount or overpay? PRO

Yes. When you tap Settle, the amount is pre-filled with the full debt but you can edit it to any value. Partial payments reduce the debt. Overpayments (e.g. rounding up on Venmo) automatically flip the balance — if Alice over-settles by $5, the ledger shows Bob now owes Alice $5.

What happens if I edit an expense after settling? PRO

If you change an expense's amount, currency, payer, or split after settlements have been recorded, Daythread warns you that existing settlements may no longer be accurate. You can proceed anyway or cancel. To clean up outdated settlements, swipe to delete the relevant settlement row in the expense list and re-settle.

Co-editing & Sync

How do I share a trip with travel companions? PRO

In the Timeline, tap the people icon in the top-right corner to open Group Sync. Tap "Invite People to This Trip" — this creates a shareable link for the trip. Once the share is active, you can send the invite link via Messages or any app.

Can co-editors change the itinerary? PRO

Yes. Editors can add, reorder, lock, and delete events just like the trip owner. Viewer-role members see the itinerary in read-only mode.

How quickly do changes appear for co-editors? PRO

Updates sync over the internet and typically appear within a few seconds. The timeline refreshes automatically in the background.

Does my data sync across my own devices?

Yes. If you're signed into the same iCloud account on multiple devices, your trips, events, documents, and expenses sync automatically. Changes propagate in the background without any manual action.

What happens if two people edit at the same time?

The app automatically merges changes made at the same time. If two people edit the exact same detail simultaneously, the most recent change is saved.

How do I add or remove people after sharing? PRO

Open Group Sync and tap "Trip is shared — Manage people & permissions". This opens the system sharing sheet where you can add people, change each person's permission (edit or view-only), or stop sharing — without having to stop and re-invite.

Why do my co-editors show as "Owner" instead of a name? PRO

That label comes from Apple's system sharing sheet, which only shows a name when you have that person saved in Contacts under their iCloud email or phone number. Daythread also keeps its own member list in Group Sync that shows real names regardless of Contacts — set yours in Settings → Your Profile → Your name. Everyone's name appears once each person has opened Group Sync once.

What happens to co-editors when I stop sharing? PRO

When you stop sharing, the trip is removed from all co-editors' devices — the same way stopping a shared Apple Note works. They lose access to the trip and its events, documents, and expenses. Before stopping, give them a heads-up so they can note anything they need.

How do I stop sharing a trip? PRO

Open Group Sync, tap "Trip is shared — Manage people & permissions", then choose "Stop Sharing" in the system sheet. Co-editors will no longer see updates.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device? PRO

Go to Profile → Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed into the same Apple ID that made the original purchase, Pro will be restored instantly.

What are ETA badges? PRO

ETA badges appear between consecutive events on the same day and show the estimated travel time — for example, "12 min walk →". They are calculated using Apple Maps and require a network connection.
